# Business Problem

## Business Context

Modern enterprises generate strategic decisions based on information from global news, geopolitical events, financial announcements, and public sentiment. However, this information is scattered across thousands of news sources, making it difficult to obtain a unified and trustworthy view of global events.

An Enterprise News Intelligence Platform can consolidate, process, and transform this data into reliable business insights that support operational, strategic, and AI-driven decision making.

---

## Problem Statement

Business users need timely access to trusted global event data for analytics and decision-making.

Traditional reporting systems are capable of answering structured questions such as event counts, trends, and regional activity, but they struggle to answer semantic questions like:

- What are today's most important global events?
- Which countries are becoming geopolitically unstable?
- Which organizations are receiving increasing media attention?
- What events are similar to this article?
- Why has AI-related news increased this week?

The absence of a unified, scalable data platform limits both traditional analytics and modern AI capabilities.

---

## Current Challenges

Organizations face several challenges when working with large-scale news and event data:

- News data is distributed across multiple sources.
- Large data volumes require scalable processing.
- Raw data contains inconsistencies and duplicate information.
- Traditional BI tools cannot perform semantic understanding.
- Building AI applications requires well-governed and enriched data.
- Managing historical and continuously arriving data efficiently is complex.

---

## Expected Business Outcomes

The proposed platform will enable organizations to:

- Build a trusted enterprise lakehouse for global event intelligence.
- Support scalable analytics using modern Data Engineering practices.
- Deliver curated datasets for dashboards and reporting.
- Enable AI-ready data through enrichment and embeddings.
- Support semantic search and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Provide a single governed data foundation for both Business Intelligence and AI applications.
