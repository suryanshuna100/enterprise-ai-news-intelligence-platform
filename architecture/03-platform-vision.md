

## Platform Vision

The Enterprise AI News Intelligence Platform aims to provide a scalable, production-grade data platform that transforms raw global event data into trusted business intelligence and AI-ready knowledge.

The platform combines modern Data Engineering, Analytics, and Artificial Intelligence to support both structured reporting and natural language exploration from a single governed data foundation.

Rather than building a traditional ETL pipeline, the objective is to create an enterprise platform that continuously evolves through versioned releases while following industry best practices in architecture, governance, scalability, and observability.

---

## Target Users

The platform is designed for multiple types of users across an enterprise.

### Business Analysts
- Analyze geopolitical trends.
- Build interactive dashboards.
- Generate business reports.

### Data Engineers
- Develop and maintain scalable data pipelines.
- Monitor data quality and pipeline execution.
- Optimize distributed processing.

### Data Scientists
- Access curated datasets for feature engineering.
- Perform trend analysis and predictive modeling.

### Business Leaders
- Monitor global events.
- Identify emerging risks.
- Support strategic decision making.

### AI Applications
- Retrieve contextual business knowledge.
- Perform semantic search.
- Answer business questions using Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## Long-Term Vision

The long-term vision is to evolve this project from an educational portfolio into a realistic enterprise Proof of Concept (POC) that demonstrates the skills expected from modern Data Engineers.

The platform will evolve through multiple releases.

### Release v1.x

Enterprise Lakehouse Platform

- Data ingestion
- Medallion Architecture
- Delta Lake
- Snowflake
- Power BI

### Release v2.x

AI Enablement Platform

- AI enrichment
- Embeddings
- Vector database
- Semantic search

### Release v3.x

Enterprise AI Platform

- AI Business Assistant
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
- Natural language analytics
- Enterprise observability
- Production deployment

Ultimately, the platform should demonstrate end-to-end Data Engineering, Analytics, and AI capabilities while following enterprise architecture principles and production engineering practices.

## Design Principles

The platform will be designed according to the following principles:

- Cloud-first architecture
- AI-ready by design
- Incremental and scalable processing
- Separation of storage and compute
- Metadata-driven engineering
- Modular and extensible architecture
- Open standards where possible
- Cost-conscious implementation
